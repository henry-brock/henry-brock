## Highlighted projects

**[Gamified Open Day Outreach Application](https://github.com/henry-brock/OpenDay-GameDemo)**: MSc dissertation, Aston University (2026)
A Unity platformer that introduces prospective students to key Computer Science modules at university
open days. Engineered end to end with an iterative and incremental process, from requirements gathering
through UML design, implementation and refinement, for visitors with no prior Computer Science knowledge.

**Engineers Without Borders: Ladywood Waste Challenge**: MSc group project, Aston University (2026)
A multidisciplinary team project tackling waste build-up in Ladywood, Birmingham. We found the core
barrier to recycling (48% of residents have no car and can't reach recycling centres) and proposed the
Ladywood Bin Hub, a local waste facility near the worst-affected terraces.

**Computational Modelling of Amyloid-Beta Protein Misfolding**: BSc dissertation, University of Bradford (2023)
Ran GROMACS molecular dynamics simulations to test whether modifying a region of the amyloid-beta
protein, which is linked to Alzheimer's disease, triggers misfolding. Analysing the large simulation
datasets showed the region stayed stable in both cases, a clear negative result that narrowed the
search for misfolding triggers.
